PARADISE

2020, 35mm film photographs transferred to HD, colour, sound, 05:13

Inspired by the pachinko chapter from Roland Barthes’ book The Empire of Signs, PARADISE explores the enmeshed relationship between labour, capitalism and play through kaleidoscopic visions of a pachinko parlour and polyphonic voices.
