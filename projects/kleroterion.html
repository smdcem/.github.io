Kleroterion (Work in progress)

2026, Super 8 transferred to HD, colour, sound, 09:26

Observed through the workers and machines of the pachinko parlour Rakuen (“Paradise”), Kleroterion examines the intersection between work and leisure, questioning what constitutes democracy today.
