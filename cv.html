CV

Born and bred in Chiba, Japan — Lives and works in London, UK

Chiemi Shimada 島田千絵美 is a London-based artist and filmmaker. Working across film, performance, installation, and workshops, she explores and interrogates memory, modernity, liminal states, and late-stage capitalism. Her work has been presented in galleries, museums and film festivals internationally, including International Film Festival Rotterdam, Open City Documentary Festival, Courtisane Festival, Arkipel, and the ICA London, Barbican, the National Archives of Singapore, Cineteca Nacional México and Kunstnernes Hus.
